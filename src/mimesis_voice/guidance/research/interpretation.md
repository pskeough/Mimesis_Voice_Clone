Separates what the data shows from what the authors infer from it, in that order. One interpretation per paragraph, tied to a specific result by name. Candidate mechanisms are labelled as candidates. Alternative explanations are named, and for each the text says whether the design rules it out, weakens it, or leaves it open.

Fails when inference is phrased as observation, when a mechanism is asserted without the result that supports it, when alternatives are omitted or dismissed without a reason, or when the interpretation would require data the study did not collect.
