Each limitation is named with the direction it pushes the finding (inflates, deflates, or unknown), whether anything in the design mitigates it, and which conclusions survive it. The ones a critical reviewer would raise first come first. A limitation that voids a claim is stated as voiding it.

Fails when a limitation is a disguised strength, when it is deferred to future work instead of assessed, when the list is generic to all studies of the type rather than specific to this design, or when a known weakness is left for the reviewer to find.
