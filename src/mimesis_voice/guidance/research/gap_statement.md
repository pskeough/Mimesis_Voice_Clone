Names what prior work examined and the specific thing it did not examine, could not distinguish, or measured without checking. States what remains unknown as a result and why that matters to a named reader. The contrast is carried by what the studies did and did not do, not by adjectives about their quality.

Fails when it claims nobody has done something without that having been checked, when it lists gaps in the plural without committing to the one this paper fills, or when the gap is a restatement of the paper's topic rather than a missing piece of knowledge.
