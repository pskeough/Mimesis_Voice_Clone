Quotes or precisely paraphrases the concern. States what changed, where (section, page, table), and gives the new text or number. Where the request is declined, the reason and the evidence for the reason follow, and the disagreement is stated as a disagreement. One concern per item, in the reviewer's order.

Fails when it thanks before it answers, when a change is described without its location, when a concern is reframed into an easier one, when the response argues where an edit would settle it, or when the tone defends the paper instead of addressing the point.
