Names who would act differently and how, tied to one specific result. The scope of the implication is set by the population, the models, the corpus, or the task actually studied, and the sentence says so. Where an implication depends on an assumption the study did not test, the assumption is stated.

Fails when the implication is for the field in general, when it recommends a policy the evidence does not reach, when it is phrased with the confidence of a result, or when it repeats the motivation from the Introduction instead of answering it.
