The first sentence states the conclusion the figure supports, as a claim. The rest says what was plotted: the quantity on each axis with units, the N behind each point or bar, what the error bars or bands are, and how the values were computed or aggregated. A reader who sees only the figure and caption can state the finding and check its basis.

Fails when the caption is a title (Results for condition A), when it repeats the body text, when it interprets beyond what is plotted, or when abbreviations in the figure are not resolved.
