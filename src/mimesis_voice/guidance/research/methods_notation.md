Each symbol is defined once, at first use, in prose and in the formula together. One symbol per concept and one concept per symbol. Notation appears only where it removes an ambiguity that prose leaves open; a formula that restates a sentence is dropped. Indices, sets, and ranges are stated with their bounds.

Fails when mathematics is used to impress rather than to specify, when a technical symbol is later used in a non-technical sense, when notation from a cited paper is imported with a different meaning, or when a theorem or definition is included that no later result depends on.
