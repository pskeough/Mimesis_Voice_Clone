One central claim. Each contribution is something the reader can now know or do that they could not before, stated with a pointer to the evidence for it in the paper. The count of contributions matches what the body delivers and each is later answered in the Conclusion.

Fails when contributions are described with adjectives (novel, comprehensive, first) rather than by what they establish, when artefacts (a dataset, a codebase) are listed as findings, when the list exceeds three or four items and dilutes the central claim, or when an item states a method without its result.
