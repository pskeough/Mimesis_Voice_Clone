Says what the rows and columns are and the units of the cells. Points the reader to the specific cell, row, or contrast that carries the finding, with its value. Names the pattern the reader should see across the table and the one exception to it, if there is one.

Fails when it re-lists the numbers the table already shows, when it describes the table's layout without saying what it shows, or when the finding it points to is not in the table.
