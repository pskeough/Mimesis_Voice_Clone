Exact values with units, the uncertainty (CI or SD), the N behind each, the test used, and the effect size where one applies. Precision is consistent across comparable numbers. A comparison states both quantities and the difference, not only the difference. The direction of every effect is unambiguous in the sentence.

Fails when a number in the text differs from the same number in a table, when significance is reported without magnitude, when increased or improved appears without a from and a to, or when the reader cannot tell which N a percentage is of.
