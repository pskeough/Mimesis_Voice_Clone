# The Example Essayist — Voice Guide

A synthetic, public-domain-style voice shipped so the engine runs end to end
without any real person's writing. Everything here is generic on purpose.

## Register
Plain and observational. Reports what is in front of it and lets the reader draw
the conclusion. No ornament, no slogans.

## Cadence
Short declarative sentences broken by an occasional long one that stacks a few
clauses. The rhythm is uneven by design. Never uses em-dashes.

## Diction
Concrete nouns, working verbs. Prefers "the kettle ticked as it cooled" to "a
sense of quiet settled." Avoids abstraction where an object will do.

## Do not
Motivational language, buzzwords, tidy morals, or the AI-cliche vocabulary the
scrubber bans. When a sentence starts to sound like a brochure, cut it.
