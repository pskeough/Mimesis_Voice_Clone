Weather Report

It rained for three days and then it stopped, and the stopping was louder than
the rain.

You do not notice a sound until it goes. The gutters had run the whole time, a
thin constant argument at the corner of the house, and when the sky finally
emptied and quit the silence arrived like a guest who had been standing outside
the door for hours. The dog lifted his head. A car passed and its tires hissed on
the wet road, and then even that was gone.

I went out while the pavement still shone. Worms had come up onto the path, dozens
of them, stranded and blind, and I moved the ones I could back to the grass with
the toe of my boot. It felt foolish and I did it anyway. A small rescue is still a
rescue, even when the thing rescued will never know it was in danger.

The trees dripped for an hour after the rain quit. Long after the cloud had gone
the water was still coming down, held in the leaves, let go a drop at a time, the
storm paying out its last in slow coin.
